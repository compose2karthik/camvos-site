Current design patterns worth using

Figma's 2026 design overview discusses bold typography, immersive elements, motion, vibrant color and collage. Those are inputs, not requirements to follow every trend.

  • Expressive typography: a strong sans-serif hierarchy with a restrained editorial serif accent.
  • Layered photography: a tactile photo and strip composition fits the actual category.
  • Useful interaction: visitors switch illustrative output formats and explore platform surfaces at their own pace.
  • Selective motion: motion previews start only on request and have a pause control; no motion sample autoplays, and reduced-motion preferences suppress decorative CSS animation.
  • Contrast and rhythm: quiet light sections alternate with navy creative storytelling and a purposeful purple accent.
  • Performance discipline: optimized local imagery and on-demand motion assets, system fonts, no heavy animation framework, no autoplay video or third-party trackers.

Image generation brief

Premium editorial event photograph of three adult friends: a South Asian woman in silver satin, a Black man in navy, and an East Asian woman in plum. Direct-camera candid joy, navy background, subtle violet and blue light, natural skin texture, tasteful distant confetti. Landscape composition suitable for portrait crops. No text, logos, devices, UI or watermarks.

Additional generation briefs

Photo motion: preserve the three fictional faces, clothes and static event camera; generate a 2×2 sequence of four distinct poses with a lean and a raised friendly wave, consistent framing and no borders/text.

Cosmic Explorers: preserve the fictional faces and group order; transform the setting into a violet/blue planet landscape with ringed planet and original silver-accented explorer outfits. No franchise characters or face-obscuring helmets.

Avatar motion: convert the same characters into original rounded 3D avatars; generate a four-frame greeting with an arm raise/wave, gentle head tilt and blink, consistent camera/design and no known franchise style.

360° generation brief and assets

Built-in image generation: create an original fictional adult guest in a formal navy suit; show eight ordered, full-body camera viewpoints at 45° steps around a fixed pose on a low platform, in a navy/violet event room. Side and rear views must change perspective and occlusion, not rotate a flat photograph. Encode a silent orbit storyboard concept with generated angles. Poster: assets/orbit-poster.webp; video: assets/orbit-preview.mp4.

How to assess the result

Can an operator identify the product, understand Experiences versus Layouts, find their platform surface, distinguish vision from availability and take a clear next step? Visual ambition should support those tasks. Market-leading design is a goal; superiority cannot be established without comparable browser reviews and user evidence.
