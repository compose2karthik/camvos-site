06 / A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Can I buy or download CAMVOS today?+

This is a private website design preview. Commercial launch, pricing and download availability have not been established.

Is every platform surface already available?+

The platform scope is approved. Accepted software baselines are separate from integrated production deployment. Each release needs its own capability and compatibility evidence.

How does CAMVOS use AI?+

AI Design supports pre-Event editable design proposals. AI Guest Experience transforms captured assets within a configured guest journey. Provider-specific quality, privacy and performance require validation.

Which cameras and printers are supported?+

Canon workflows have reported physical test evidence. A complete supported-model, operating-system and printer matrix will be published after release verification.

Where do social account credentials live?+

Social connections and publishing belong to CAMVOS Cloud/Delivery. Desktop can request a publishing action, but provider tokens and secrets stay in secure server-side storage.
