Good questions. Clear answers.

Can I buy or download CAMVOS today?+

CAMVOS is in development. A commercial release, download and pricing have not been approved.

Which formats are available?+

Photo, Collage, Strip, GIF and Boomerang have previously reported Desktop validation. This website is not fresh release evidence. Video, 360 and AI workflows require release-specific verification; every sample shown here is illustrative.

Which hardware is supported?+

A verified camera, printer and operating-system compatibility list will be published with the relevant release. Do not purchase hardware based solely on this preview.

Can I capture without internet?+

Local event execution is a design requirement. Cloud AI, hosted galleries and connected delivery can still need internet; offline behavior must be validated for each release.

How does AI fit into CAMVOS?+

The vision includes design assistance before an event and curated guest transformations during it. Provider availability, privacy, quality and usage costs require verification.

How can I get updates?+

Update registration is not open yet. The Contact page explains the current state and lets you prepare a local inquiry brief. Nothing is sent.
