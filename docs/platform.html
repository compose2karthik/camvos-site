CAMVOS Desktop

The local operator application for event preparation, creative design, supported hardware, capture and live sessions. Live capture is designed to remain local instead of relying on venue connectivity.

Platform vision · Integration and deployment unverified

CAMVOS Owner Web

The broader business-management environment for organizations, events, clients, commercial records and team operations. Its integration with live event operations requires separate verification.

Planned / developing · Availability unverified

CAMVOS Mobile & iPad

The vision includes roaming capture, event companions and selected booth or 360 participation. Hardware, permissions and offline use depend on the supported device and release.

In development · Hosted gallery availability unverified

CAMVOS Guest

Guest choices, capture, results and configured delivery. Local guest/kiosk services are distinct from a hosted gallery; one does not establish the availability of the other.

Platform vision · Production deployment unverified

CAMVOS Cloud

Connected account, entitlement, fleet, release and coordination services. These support the business around local operation; not every service is integrated or publicly available.
